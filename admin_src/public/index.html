Audio files

Shared by all of the account's stations

It is best to upload a file that is already in the station's stream format (the same format, bitrate, sample rate and mono or stereo; MP3 at a constant bitrate; AAC as an .aac file): it is then played exactly as you made it. A file in another format (WAV, FLAC, M4A, Ogg, or MP3/AAC with other settings) can be converted for you, if you agree to it: it is re-encoded, which costs a little quality when the upload was already compressed, its loudness is matched to the stream, and the converted file takes the place of the one you uploaded.

Prices

A station is charged for the listeners it is allowed, at its stream's bitrate, and an account for its storage. The price of one listener is what a server costs you divided by the listeners such a server can carry at that bitrate, plus your margin. Leave the server cost at 0 to show no prices. A discount or a fixed price can be set per station, and a discount per account.

Email

Optional. With a mail server entered here, accounts that have given an address are sent notices about their limits and subscriptions. Without it, nothing is sent and everything else works the same.
