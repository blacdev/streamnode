Files

Shared by all of the account's stations

It is best to upload a file that is already in the station's stream format (the same format, bitrate, sample rate and mono or stereo; MP3 at a constant bitrate; AAC as an .aac file): it is then played exactly as you made it. A file in another format (WAV, FLAC, M4A, Ogg, or MP3/AAC with other settings) can be converted for you, if you agree to it: it is re-encoded, which costs a little quality when the upload was already compressed, its loudness is matched to the stream, and the converted file takes the place of the one you uploaded. Station images (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF) are kept here too and count toward the same storage. To prepare files yourself, StreamNode Converter (Windows and Linux) converts any audio or video file to a stream's format on your own computer: download for Windows or for Linux.

Prices

A station is charged for the listeners it is allowed, at its stream's bitrate, and an account for its storage. The price of one listener is what a server costs you divided by the listeners such a server can carry at that bitrate, plus your margin. Leave the server cost at 0 to show no prices. A discount or a fixed price can be set per station, and a discount per account.

Pay as you go

For stations and accounts set to pay as you go: what is used beyond the subscription is let through and charged, instead of being refused.
